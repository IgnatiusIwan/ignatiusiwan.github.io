FedCLLM is an approach using Large Language Models (LLM) proficiency in semantic tasks on text-based data to compare client and server domain descriptions summary in a text format and assess their similarity. Experiments on popular benchmark datasets show that FedCLLM effectively distinguishes honest clients from potentially malicious ones and outperforms other previous works in terms of performance. You can refer to the original paper {% cite iwan2025fedcllm %}.
